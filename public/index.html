Frequently asked questions

How Cognis handles evidence, live data, and investment decisions.

What does Cognis do?＋

Cognis turns venture information into a structured diligence record: intake details, assessment questions, evidence states, risks, and a committee-ready recommendation draft.

Is the information live or demonstration data?＋

The starter ventures are clearly seeded demonstration records. New ventures and analyst responses are stored in the local database, while supported token evidence is requested live from Binance through the Cognis backend.

How does Cognis reach a recommendation?＋

It derives progress and blockers from applicable assessment questions, then keeps supporting evidence, missing information, and contradictions visible. It does not turn uncertainty into an unexplained score.

What is Binance Agent OS used for?＋

The Binance integration supplies token search, market details, rankings, and security-audit evidence. Cognis normalizes those responses and attaches them to the relevant diligence context.

Does Cognis make the final investment decision?＋

No. Cognis prepares an evidence-backed advance, hold, or pass direction. The analyst and investment committee retain responsibility for the final decision and rationale.
